# WFH Tracker Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 17 August 2026

## 1\. About this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how WFH Tracker ("we", "us", or "our") handles personal information when you use the WFH Tracker application at https://wfhtracker.dcdigital.au/.

WFH Tracker is a project operated by David Cass under the DC Digital brand.

## 2\. Information we collect

WFH Tracker may collect:

* **Account information**, including the email address associated with your account and information provided when creating or accessing your account.
* **WFH records**, including information you enter into the application about working from home.
* **Calculation-related information**, including information used by the application to estimate working-from-home tax deductions.
* **Technical information** necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the application.

You choose what WFH information to enter into the application.

## 3\. How we use information

We use information to:

* provide and operate WFH Tracker;
* authenticate and manage your account;
* store and retrieve your WFH records;
* perform calculations and display estimates within the application;
* maintain security and prevent misuse;
* troubleshoot and improve the application; and
* comply with applicable legal obligations.

WFH Tracker does not sell your personal information.

## 4\. Tax information and calculations

WFH Tracker provides estimates based on information entered by the user and the calculation method implemented by the application.

WFH Tracker is not a tax agent and does not provide personal tax, financial, or accounting advice.

You are responsible for checking calculations and determining whether information is appropriate for your own circumstances and tax return.

## 5\. How information is stored

Account and application data is stored using cloud services used to operate WFH Tracker.

Access to application data is designed to be restricted to the authenticated user associated with that data.

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

## 6\. Authentication and third-party services

WFH Tracker uses third-party cloud and authentication services to provide account authentication, application hosting, storage, and related functionality.

These providers may process information on our behalf and may have their own privacy policies.

## 7\. Disclosure of information

We may disclose personal information:

* to service providers necessary to operate WFH Tracker;
* where required or authorised by law; or
* where reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or property of DC Digital, WFH Tracker, or its users.

We do not sell personal information.

## 8\. Cookies and analytics

WFH Tracker may use cookies or browser storage necessary for authentication, security, application functionality, or maintaining an authenticated session.

We do not use information entered into WFH Tracker to provide targeted advertising.

## 9\. Data retention and deletion

Your account and application data may be retained while your account is active.

If you want to request deletion of your account or application data, contact DC Digital using the details below. We will consider and action the request subject to applicable legal or technical requirements.

## 10\. Access and correction

You may contact us to request access to, or correction of, personal information we hold about you, subject to applicable law.

**Email:** contact@dcdigital.au

## 11\. International disclosure

Some technology and service providers used to operate WFH Tracker may process or store information outside Australia.

Where applicable, we take reasonable steps to address the handling of personal information by those providers in accordance with applicable Australian privacy requirements.

## 12\. Children

WFH Tracker is not specifically directed to children. Users should not create an account or use the application if they are unable to agree to the applicable Terms of Use.

## 13\. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
